Why the name Zebra Tap

Zebra striping

Zebra striping is something people already do: alternate a beer with an alcohol-free drink through the night. It's one of the drinking habits growing fastest in France.

The name comes from that. Black stripes for the rounds with alcohol, white stripes for the ones without.

Today, the white stripes usually mean switching to a bottle or a soft drink. With Zebra Tap, every round is the same beer.
